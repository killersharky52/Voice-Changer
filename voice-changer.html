About Formant: pitch alone makes a "chipmunk," not a different gender — the real cue is formant structure (the resonance pattern set by vocal tract shape). This control now cuts your voice's natural resonance at its original frequencies and boosts a new one at the shifted target, so it actually overrides the old pattern instead of just adding a little color next to it. Push the slider toward the ends (±80–100%) for a clearly audible change. It's still DSP filtering, not AI voice conversion — "noticeably more convincing," not "indistinguishable."
Note: this runs entirely in your browser — audio never leaves your device. It works like an app: add this page to your phone's home screen for a full-screen icon. For true feedback-free speaker playback, wired or Bluetooth headphones are strongly recommended.
