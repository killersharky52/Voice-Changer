About Formant: pitch alone makes a "chipmunk," not a different gender — the real cue is formant structure (the resonance pattern set by vocal tract shape). This control cuts your voice's natural resonance at its original frequencies and boosts a new one at the shifted target. Push the slider toward the ends (±80–100%) for a clearly audible change. It's still DSP filtering, not AI voice conversion.
Note: this runs entirely in your browser — audio never leaves your device. It works like an app: add this page to your phone's home screen for a full-screen icon. For true feedback-free speaker playback, wired or Bluetooth headphones are strongly recommended.
Presets: dial in a sound, then tap + Save Preset to name and add it to the list. Custom presets show a small × to delete them; the seven built-in presets can't be removed. Saved presets are stored locally in this browser, so they'll still be here next time you open this file on this device — but won't follow you to a different browser or computer.
